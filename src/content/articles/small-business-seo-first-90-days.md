SEO (search engine optimization) can feel like a black box. It doesn't have to be. Most small businesses get the biggest wins from a handful of basics done well. Here's a realistic plan for your first three months.

## Month 1: Get the foundations right

**Set up Google Business Profile.** If you serve local customers, this is the single most important thing you can do. Fill out every section: services, hours, service area, photos, and a description that says plainly what you do and where.

**Connect Google Search Console.** It's free, and it shows which searches your site appears for and whether Google has found problems with your pages.

**Fix the basics on every page.** Each page should have:

- A unique title that says what the page is about (for example, "Website Design for Restaurants in Toronto").
- A short description that makes someone want to click.
- One clear main heading.
- Text that answers what a customer actually wants to know.

**Check your speed and mobile experience.** Most of your visitors are on a phone. If your site is slow or awkward on a small screen, people leave, and Google notices.

## Month 2: Build pages people are searching for

**Give each service its own page.** One "Services" page listing everything is hard for Google to rank. A dedicated page for each main service gives you a much better chance.

**Answer common questions.** Think about what customers ask you on the phone. Each of those questions can become a helpful article. That's exactly why we publish a new article every week.

**Make your contact details consistent.** Your business name, address, and phone number should be identical on your website, Google profile, and directories like Yelp or Facebook.

## Month 3: Earn trust

**Ask for reviews.** Reviews help you rank in local results and help people choose you. Make it easy: send happy customers a direct link to your Google review page.

**Get a few quality links.** When other reputable websites link to yours, Google sees it as a vote of confidence. Local associations, suppliers, partners, and local news are great places to start. We cover this in detail in [What Are Backlinks?](/articles/what-are-backlinks)

**Review what's working.** Look at Search Console. Which pages are getting seen? Which searches are you close to ranking for? Do more of what's working.

## How long until you see results?

Honest answer: SEO is a slow build. Small wins, like your Google Business Profile appearing in local searches, can happen in weeks. Ranking for competitive searches usually takes several months of steady work. Anyone promising page one in a week is selling something you shouldn't buy.

## What to skip

- Stuffing keywords into every sentence.
- Buying hundreds of cheap links.
- Paying for "guaranteed rankings."

These either don't work or can get you penalized.
