## What is LinoCon Books?

LinoCon Books is a planned bookkeeping application from LinoCon Digital, intended for small businesses that want a practical way to organize key financial workflows. The planned experience brings everyday bookkeeping information together so owners can more easily review money coming in, money going out, invoices, expenses, and tax-related figures.

Rather than presenting it as a universal solution for every business, LinoCon Books is being developed around common small-business needs. Businesses should assess the final product against their own transaction volume, accounting requirements, tax obligations, and existing workflows once full product details are published.

## Planned features

### 1. A dashboard for financial visibility

The planned dashboard is intended to bring together key figures such as money in, money out, profit, and GST-related amounts. A dashboard can make it easier to spot trends and questions that need attention, but it is only as accurate as the records and settings behind it. It should support (not replace) regular reconciliation and professional accounting advice when needed.

### 2. Branded invoices

The planned app includes branded invoices with business identity, applicable GST details, and a payment button. Consistent invoices can help present a professional experience and make it easier for customers to understand what they owe and how to pay. Businesses will still need to confirm that invoice content and tax details comply with the requirements that apply to them.

### 3. Expense tracking with receipt photos

The planned expense workflow includes recording expenses and attaching receipt photos. Keeping supporting documents connected to transactions can make records easier to retrieve later. Owners must still enter accurate information, use sensible categories, and retain records in accordance with applicable rules.

### 4. GST reports for filing periods

The product is planned to include GST-related reports organized by filing period. Reports can help assemble information for review, but they do not guarantee that a return is correct. Registration status, transaction coding, eligibility, and tax treatment should be checked before filing.

### 5. Exports for an accountant

The planned product includes clean exports intended to help businesses share records with an accountant. Before adopting any bookkeeping tool, ask which file formats are supported, what information is included, and whether the export is useful to your accounting professional.

### 6. Secure sign-in

The planned experience includes secure login. Security is not a single feature or promise: it depends on the final implementation, access controls, hosting, maintenance, backups, and how users protect their credentials. Final security details should be reviewed when the product is available.

## A different approach to payment and ownership

One feature of the planned LinoCon Books model is its defined payment period. The current plan is for customers to pay over 12 months. After the 12-month term, customers who choose to keep the app hosted with LinoCon Digital would pay a small ongoing hosting fee.

The intended model is that customers own the app after completing the payment period and can take it with them if they leave. The exact handover process, technical requirements, third-party dependencies, exclusions, and final terms will need to be clearly documented before launch. Ownership does not necessarily mean every external service, domain, hosting environment, integration, or third-party licence transfers automatically.

That distinction matters. When comparing software options, ask what you own, what remains a service, what recurring charges may apply, what happens if you stop paying, and whether you can export or move your information.

## Who might benefit from a tool like this?

A bookkeeping app may be worth considering for a small business that needs a more consistent way to issue invoices, track expenses, store receipt records, review cash flow, or prepare information for an accountant. It may be particularly useful for owners who currently rely on scattered documents or manual spreadsheets and want a more organized workflow.

However, no single app fits every business. A company with complex inventory, payroll, multi-currency operations, advanced reporting, or specialized accounting requirements may need a different system or additional integrations. The right choice depends on the business, not just the product's marketing.

## What small businesses should check before choosing any bookkeeping app

- Does it support your invoicing, expense, reporting, and tax workflows?
- Can you export your records in formats your accountant can use?
- How are data security, access permissions, backups, and recovery handled?
- What is included in the price, and what costs extra?
- What happens to your app and data if you stop using the provider?
- Are integrations, payment processing, hosting, and third-party licences included or separate?
- Who provides support and maintenance, and what are the response expectations?
- Does the system fit your legal, tax, and record-retention requirements?

Request written answers and review the agreement before committing. Compare total cost over several years, not only the initial payment or monthly price.
